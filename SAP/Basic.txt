> What ERP does?

   ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) system integrate business applications to manage a company's operations, including finance, human resources, manufacturing
  ,Supply chain and more. It is a centralized system that streamline operations by coordinating data flow between processes.

   Software to manage companies resources is called ERP.

   The most popular and widely used ERP software is SAP. SAP is a multinational company that sells ERP software solutions
   to business.It groups process into modules and interact with different business aspects.


> SAP Project types;

    . Implementation Project - Implementing SAP in an organization
    . Rollout Project - SAP implemented in one of the business process and is planning to expand in other process/organization branch
    . Support Project - For end-user support who faces issues while using sap services
